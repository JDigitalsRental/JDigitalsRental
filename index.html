NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What rental plans are available?

We offer weekly rentals at regular game prices and monthly UNLI SWAP packages.

Monthly UNLI SWAP (Trophy): ₱850
Monthly UNLI SWAP (Non-Trophy): ₱850

How many times can I swap games?

Only Monthly UNLI SWAP includes game swaps. All other rental plans have no swaps.

Monthly UNLI SWAP allows unlimited swaps, maximum 1 swap per week.

Can I play multiple games at the same time?

No. Only one game at a time is allowed under the rental plan.

Are game swaps guaranteed?

Game swaps depend on availability.

How do I reserve a game?

Click Rent Now, complete the rental form, and send the details through Messenger.

Can I play using my own PS5 profile?

It depends on your selected account type. We will provide the correct setup instructions.
